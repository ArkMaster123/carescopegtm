- "caring for mom with dementia"
- "first time caregiver"

- "choosing assisted living"

#### Browse Related Content
- Check comments on popular caregiving videos
- Look at who engages with larger influencers
- Explore TikTok's "For You" page after engaging with caregiving content
- Check video duets and stitches of popular creators

## 💡 Strategy Recommendations for CareScope Intelligence

### Why Micro-Influencers?

1. **Higher Engagement**: 8.7% average engagement vs 2.9% for mega-influencers
2. **Authentic Stories**: More relatable, casual, and genuine content
3. **Budget-Friendly**: More accessible for startup partnerships
4. **Niche Audience**: Followers are highly targeted and engaged
5. **Trend**: By 2027, 80% of TikTok marketing budgets will go to creators under 100K followers

### Partnership Approach

**Content Ideas:**
- "How I found the right care for my mom" story
- "Questions to ask when choosing a care facility" (featuring CareScope)
- "My experience using CareScope to register my dad"
- Day-in-the-life content showing care research process
- Tips for families navigating care decisions

**Messaging Focus:**
- Emotional support for difficult decisions
- Practical guidance for finding quality care
- Community building among caregivers
- Transparency in care facility selection
- Peace of mind for families

### Outreach Tips

1. **Personalize**: Reference specific content they've created
2. **Align Values**: Emphasize CareScope's mission to help families
3. **Offer Value**: Provide early access, affiliate opportunities, or genuine support
4. **Build Relationships**: Engage authentically before pitching
5. **Respect Their Audience**: Let creators maintain their authentic voice
